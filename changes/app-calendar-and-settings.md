The calendar and every settings page of the app now do what they show. The calendar has day, week, month and year views; click an empty slot or drag over a stretch of time to name a new entry in a small box ("More options" opens the whole form), drag an entry to another time or day, drag its lower edge to change its length, and take any change back with Undo or Z. A click on an entry shows who put it there, what was done and how it ended; a line marks the current time, entries at the same time stand side by side, and entries of a whole day or longer sit in a row on top. The calendar has its own keys (D W M Y, T, J/N, K/P, G, C, E, Delete, Z, /, R), listed with the others under Ctrl+/. Settings › Usage is a page of activity: a time range, totals, charts by day and tables by chat, kind and model; calls under your own plan are counted as calls, with the list price beside them and never added to what was spent. Settings › Connectors lists channels, MCP servers, external agents and skill sources, each with add, test and remove; adding a channel first asks which kind, from the channel packages installed, then follows the steps that package declares. Settings › Model applies a new default only after one test call, lists the saved models with enable, disable and remove, and shows the sources found on this computer. Packages can be searched in the registry, installed, updated and removed from the page; what the agent learned can be read, compared with the version before, undone and deleted; and Data shares with a preview, exports a card, backs up, and deletes an identity after showing what goes with it. With several identities, pages of an identity's own things have "Applies to" at the top. Setting names and choices come from the packages that own them, in both languages.

应用的日历和每一个设置页现在都能真正用起来。日历有日、周、月、年四种视图；点一个空的时间格或拖出一段时间，就能在小框里给新的事起名（"更多选项"打开完整表单），把一件事拖到别的时间或别的日子，拖它的下边缘改时长，任何改动都能用"撤销"或 Z 收回。点一件事能看到是谁放的、做了什么、结果如何；一条线标出当前时间，同一时间的事并排显示，全天和跨天的事放在顶上一行。日历有自己的按键（D W M Y、T、J/N、K/P、G、C、E、Delete、Z、/、R），和其他快捷键一起列在 Ctrl+/ 里。设置 › 用量是一页活动记录：时间范围、合计、按天的图表，以及按对话、种类和模型的表格；用你自己的订阅发出的调用按次数计，旁边显示标价，但不会算进花费。设置 › 连接器列出渠道、MCP 服务、外部 agent 和技能来源，每一类都能添加、测试和移除；添加渠道时先从已安装的渠道包里选种类，再按那个包声明的步骤走。设置 › 模型在试过一次调用之后才应用新的默认模型，列出保存的模型并可启用、停用、移除，还显示在这台电脑上找到的来源。扩展包可以在页面里搜索注册表、安装、更新和移除；它学到的东西可以查看、和上一个版本对比、撤销和删除；数据页可以带预览地分享、导出角色卡、备份，以及在列出会一起删掉的东西之后删除身份。有多个身份时，属于身份自己的页面顶上有"作用于"。设置的名字和选项来自拥有它们的包，中英文都有。
