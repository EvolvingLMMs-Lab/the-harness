The units of reflexes are no longer told to the model as its own programs. A reflex that ran was listed as "a program of yours", the hint to queue the next program named it, and the note that a program ended was left out while a reflex ran; the model watched and cancelled reflexes as if they were its work.
<!-- zh -->
反射的单元不再被当成模型自己的程序告诉它。以前正在跑的反射会被列为"你的程序"，排队下一个程序的提示也指向它，有反射在跑时还会漏掉"程序已结束"的提示；模型于是把反射当成自己的工作去盯着、去取消。
