The page on how a tree of reflexes is made says what a trial is for: a node has one plain goal (what is the case when its situation is over and stays over), its condition holds for as long as that is not so, its answer is written for the hardest form of the situation, and its trial checks the goal in one ordinary case. A node that reached its goal has passed; nothing is added to the goal afterwards. The whole tree is set first, each node with its one trial, before the nodes that did not pass are repaired.

讲反射树怎么建的那一页说明了试验是为了什么：一个节点只有一个说得清的目标（它的情况过去并且保持过去时，什么成立），节点的条件在这一点还没成立时一直成立，应对照这种情况最难的样子来写，试验用一个普通的情况检查这个目标。达到目标的节点就算通过，之后不再往目标上加东西。先把整棵树都设上，每个节点带一次试验，再回头修没通过的节点。
