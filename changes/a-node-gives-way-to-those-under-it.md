A reflex that tells the model and ends what runs (`do="EVENT", ends=True`) no longer ends the answer of a node under it: like every node, it has its turn only when no node under it, or before it at its place, is answering. A node under another has done its work when its situation is over, which includes the conditions of the nodes above it: a node that says only what distinguishes it within its parent's situation is no longer counted as failed because its own condition still holds once the parent's is over. A reflex set with a condition and nothing to do is no longer silent: while its condition holds and no node under it answers, the model is told (the event `UNANSWERED`) and what runs is ended. A situation is in the tree from the moment its condition is set, such a node needs no trial, and the model's turn says how many situations have no answer of their own. A tree that relied on a silent grouping node gives that node a program or puts its condition into the nodes under it.

“告诉模型并结束正在运行的东西”的反射（`do="EVENT", ends=True`）不再把它下面节点的应对结束掉：和所有节点一样，只有它下面、以及同一位置排在它前面的节点都没在应对时才轮到它。挂在别的节点下面的节点，处境过去了就算完成，这包括它上面节点的条件：只写了自己特有条件的子节点，不会因为父节点的处境过去后自己的条件还成立而被算作失败。只有条件、没有动作的反射不再沉默：它的条件成立而下面没有节点应对时，会告诉模型（`UNANSWERED` 事件）并结束正在运行的东西。处境从条件设上的那一刻起就在树里，这样的节点不需要试验，每一轮都会告诉模型有几个处境还没有自己的应对。原先依赖沉默分组节点的树，要给那个节点一个程序，或者把它的条件写进下面的节点。
