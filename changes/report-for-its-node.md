The guides say what a report is for instead of what its function takes: a node's `report` is the message read when the model is told of that node, written for that node's situation (what it will need then to judge whether the situation is over and to decide what to do next); a program's `until(report=)` likewise for that program. How the function is called is said after that. The sentence that named pictures as an addition to "the values that show the situation" is gone.
