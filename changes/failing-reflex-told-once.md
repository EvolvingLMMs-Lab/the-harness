A reflex that keeps failing is told to the model once until it has done its work, also when its situation comes and goes; before, each new failure was told again and set the model's call aside. An event that repeats one the model has not answered yet sets no call aside either.
<!-- zh -->
一个持续失败的反射，在它成功完成一次之前只告诉模型一次，即使它的情况时有时无也一样；此前每次新的失败都会再报一次，并把模型正在进行的调用放到一边。重复一条模型尚未回应的事件，也不再把调用放到一边。
