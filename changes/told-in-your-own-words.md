A node of the tree of reflexes and a program say for themselves what the model reads when it is told of them. A node that tells the model anything now needs `about=` (one sentence: the situation it answers) and `report=` (a function of the view and of what its program returned) and is refused without them; only a node with `tell="never"` and `tell_failed="never"` goes without. A program may give `until(condition, report=..., interrupted=...)`: `report` is told whenever the model hears of the program, and `interrupted` says what happens when a reflex takes the environment from it: `"tell"` (the default: it goes on and the model is told, three times for one program, the rest with its end), `"stop"` (it ends there) or `"never"`. The message about a reflex is now in words instead of a record: the node with its sentence, which program it took the environment from and how often, the node's report, and that program's own report.

反射树的节点和程序自己决定模型被告知时读到什么。会告诉模型任何事的节点现在必须带 `about=`（一句话：它管的是什么处境）和 `report=`（视图和节点程序返回值的函数），否则拒绝；只有 `tell="never"` 且 `tell_failed="never"` 的节点可以不带。程序可以写 `until(condition, report=..., interrupted=...)`：`report` 的返回值在模型每次听到这个程序时都会带上；`interrupted` 说明被反射拿走环境时怎么办：`"tell"`（默认：程序继续并告诉模型，同一个程序最多单独告诉三次，其余随它的结束一起告诉）、`"stop"`（在那里结束）、`"never"`。关于反射的通知现在是一段话，不再是一条原始记录：节点和它的那句话、它从哪个程序手里拿走了环境以及第几次、节点的 report、那个程序自己的 report。
