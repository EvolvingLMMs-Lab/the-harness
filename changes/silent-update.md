On Windows the desktop app installs an update without showing the installer: the window closes, the update installs with nothing on screen, and the app reopens on the chat that was open. Before, every update opened the installer's own window with its progress page.
<!-- zh -->
在 Windows 上，桌面 app 安装更新时不再出现安装器：窗口关掉，更新在后台静默装好，然后 app 回到原来打开的对话。之前每次更新都会弹出安装器自己的窗口和进度页。
