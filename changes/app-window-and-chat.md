The app's window and chat are rebuilt for everyday use. The reply appears as it is written, tool activity shows live and then folds into one line that opens, and replies render as Markdown. Messages sent while it works wait in a queue above the composer, where each can be edited, deleted or sent now; Stop or Esc stops the turn. Unsent drafts and the reading position are kept per chat, files can be dropped or pasted as attachments, Up and Down recall sent messages, and Ctrl+F finds in the chat. A failed turn shows a card with the reason and Retry, Switch model, Open logs and Copy error; an approval asks "Do you want to proceed?" and takes 1, 2 or Esc. The model picker beside the composer changes the model of the open chat only, and a status bar under it shows what waits for approval, the context in use, the model and today's spending. The chat list has search, pin, archive, rename and delete. Ctrl+K opens a command palette and Ctrl+/ lists the shortcuts (Ctrl+, Settings, Ctrl+N new chat, Ctrl+B sidebar, Ctrl+J tools, Ctrl+Shift+F search chats). Every screen now says when it is loading, empty or failed (with the reason, the fix and Try again), every icon has a name, the whole app works from the keyboard, settings show named choices instead of raw values and say "Saved" or why not, and the fonts load. The identity's own settings are now saved for the identity; before, a refusal was silently saved as a global setting.

应用的窗口和对话为日常使用重做了一遍。回复边写边显示，工具活动实时出现、做完后收成能展开的一行，回复按 Markdown 渲染。它做事时发的消息在输入框上方排队，每一条都能编辑、删除或立即发送；停止按钮或 Esc 停下当前回合。没发出去的草稿和阅读位置按对话保存，文件可以拖进来或粘贴成附件，上下键调出发过的消息，Ctrl+F 在对话里查找。回合失败时出现一张卡片，写明原因，并带着重试、换模型、打开日志和复制错误；批准请求问"要继续吗？"，按 1、2 或 Esc 回答。输入框旁边的模型选择器只换当前对话的模型，下面的状态条显示哪些事要先问你、上下文用量、模型和今天的花费。对话列表有搜索、置顶、归档、改名和删除。Ctrl+K 打开命令面板，Ctrl+/ 列出快捷键（Ctrl+, 设置，Ctrl+N 新对话，Ctrl+B 左栏，Ctrl+J 工具，Ctrl+Shift+F 搜索对话）。每一屏现在都会说明自己正在加载、是空的还是出了错（带原因、修法和重试），每个图标都有名字，整个应用只用键盘也能操作，设置里显示的是有名字的选项而不是原始取值，并且会显示"已保存"或没保存的原因，字体也能正常加载。身份自己的设置现在保存到这个身份上；以前被拒绝时会被悄悄存成全局设置。
