The terminal chat's full-screen views do what the app's pages do, on the same data. `/calendar` shows a day, a week or a month and creates, edits, removes and undoes entries (`d` `w` `m`, `t`, `n` `p`, `c`, `e`, `x`, `z`). `/usage` covers today, 7 days and 30 days by day, chat, task and model. `/channels`, `/mcp`, `/acp` and `/skills sources` are one list of connectors, each added, tested and removed the same way; a channel is drawn from what its package declares. `/config` lists settings by their names and pages with search, `/agent` manages identities, `/library` gains a skill's diff, approve, restore and delete, `/compact` frees context, `/agents` opens or stops a sub-agent, and `/rename` without a name names the conversation by how it began. `harness "a sentence"` sends it as the first message. Changed: `/usage` opens a view in an identity's chat, and commands of a session with another agent (`/steer`, `/terminal`, `/answer`, `/merge`, `/handoff`) are offered only there.

终端聊天的全屏视图做的事和 app 的页面一样，用的是同一份数据。`/calendar` 显示一天、一周或一个月，可以新建、编辑、删除和撤销（`d` `w` `m`、`t`、`n` `p`、`c`、`e`、`x`、`z`）。`/usage` 显示今天、7 天、30 天，按天、按对话、按事、按模型。`/channels`、`/mcp`、`/acp` 和 `/skills sources` 是同一张连接器列表，每一类都用同样的方式添加、测试、删除；渠道按它的包声明的内容来画。`/config` 按名字和页面列出设置并支持搜索，`/agent` 管理身份，`/library` 增加了技能的改动对比、批准、恢复和删除，`/compact` 腾出上下文，`/agents` 可以打开或停掉一个子 agent，`/rename` 不带名字时按对话的开头起名。`harness "一句话"` 把它作为第一条消息发出。有变化的地方：在身份的对话里 `/usage` 打开的是视图；与其它 agent 的会话相关的命令（`/steer`、`/terminal`、`/answer`、`/merge`、`/handoff`）只在那种会话里出现。
