The model can take an entry out of its library: library.retire(kind, name, why), with the reason recorded in the index and the texts kept in the history. The learning pages tell it to keep one entry per subject and to correct or retire an entry that is wrong or out of date instead of adding another beside it.
<!-- zh -->
模型可以把条目从自己的库里撤下：library.retire(kind, name, why)，原因记在索引里，文本保留在历史中。学习页要求它每个主题只保留一条，条目错了或过时了就改写或撤下，而不是在旁边再加一条。
