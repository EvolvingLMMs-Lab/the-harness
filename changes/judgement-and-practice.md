Three principles in the prompts, from what learning runs did. Before starting something that lasts or going somewhere new, say what can happen there that needs an answer sooner than the model would give one, and answer the kind a thing belongs to, not only the thing that happened. A reflex acts in the model's place, so its condition is the model's whole judgement and not a signal: it holds only where acting is right at that moment. Practice time is for what does not work yet: the hardest and the costliest first, in a situation set up directly where the environment allows it.

提示词里新增三条原则，来自学习运行的实际表现。开始一件持续较久的事或去一个新地方之前，先说清那里可能发生哪些需要比模型更快响应的事；出了事要回应它所属的那一类，而不只是发生的那一件。反射是替模型行动的，所以它的条件是模型的完整判断而不是一个信号：只有在那一刻出手是对的时候才成立。练习时间留给还做不成的事：最难、代价最大的先来，环境允许时直接布置出要练的情境。
