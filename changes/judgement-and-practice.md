Three principles in the prompts, from what learning runs did. Before starting something that lasts or entering a situation it has not been in, say what can happen in it that needs an answer sooner than the model would give one, and answer the kind a thing belongs to, not only the thing that happened. A reflex acts in the model's place, so its condition is the model's whole judgement and not a signal: it holds only where acting is right at that moment. Practice time is for what does not work yet: from the ground up, each part made reliable before what depends on it, with time in proportion to how likely a part is to fail and what its failure costs, in a situation set up directly where the environment allows it.

提示词里新增三条原则，来自学习运行的实际表现。开始一件持续较久的事或进入一个没经历过的情况之前，先说清其中可能发生哪些需要比模型更快响应的事；出了事要回应它所属的那一类，而不只是发生的那一件。反射是替模型行动的，所以它的条件是模型的完整判断而不是一个信号：只有在那一刻出手是对的时候才成立。练习时间留给还做不成的事：自下而上，每一部分做到可靠再去学依赖它的；一个部分花多少时间与它失败的可能和代价成比例；环境允许时直接布置出要练的情境。
