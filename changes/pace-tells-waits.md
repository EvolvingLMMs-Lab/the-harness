The model is told, with the pace of its last reply, how many times and how long it waited for programs of its own to end since its last program, so that it sees programs that are too long as it sees ones that are too short. The learning page says a way is learned when it worked from what the steps before it leave, with nothing added, run at the pace of any work.

模型在得知上一条回复的节奏时，也会得知自上一个程序以来它为等自己的程序结束等了几次、共多少秒，这样程序太长和太短它都看得到。学习页改为：一种做法从前面步骤真正留下的状态出发、不添加任何东西做成了，才算学会；练习时和平时干活一样按节奏分程序。
