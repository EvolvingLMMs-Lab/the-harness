In the terminal chat, `/config` puts each setting on the page the app has it on, leaves out the ones nobody is meant to see and lists the advanced ones last; `/calendar` searches with `/` and marks an entry put in the past as missed; and adding a model from `/model` tries it without keeping anything until you say so.
<!-- zh -->
终端聊天里，`/config` 把每项设置放在 app 里它所在的那一页，不再列出不该给人看的设置，高级项排在最后；`/calendar` 用 `/` 搜索，并把放在过去的条目标为错过；从 `/model` 添加模型时先试一次、不保存任何东西，你确认之后才保存。
