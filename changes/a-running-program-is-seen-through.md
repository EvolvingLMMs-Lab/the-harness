What the model is told when its turn comes back with a program still running, and the core prompt with it, now say what a running program asks of it: know how long it has to run, do not wait for its end to learn that it failed, come back to look well before it is due to end, have its report show how far it is, and prepare what comes after it between the looks. Before, the message asked the model to follow the program two seconds after it started, when there was nothing to follow yet.
